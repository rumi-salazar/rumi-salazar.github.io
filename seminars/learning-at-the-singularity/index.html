Learning at the Singularity Mini-Seminar

AI systems now match human experts in many domains, and have produced claimed proofs of open problems such as Navier–Stokes. It is not clear, however, how aligned their goals and learned values are with the intentions of their designers, as the recent Hugging Face incident illustrates, nor how this gap will change as they become more capable. This is the alignment problem, and it forms a central motivation for this mini-seminar.

Modern AI systems are grown, not built, so aligning them requires understanding how they learn, which will likely need deep mathematical theory. This mini-seminar introduces mathematicians to the mathematics involved in some current approaches to alignment, including singular learning theory and neuroalgebraic geometry.
